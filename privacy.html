Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Name Tracing Hub is a browser-based worksheet generator for parents and teachers. You do not need an account or login to use it. This policy explains what happens to names entered into the generator, how your browser remembers Pro access, and which outside services are involved when you load fonts or make a payment.

Names stay in your browser

Names entered into the single-name field or batch field are processed only in your own browser. The generator does not upload those names to our servers or any other server. It does not send worksheet names to Google or Stripe. We do not maintain a database of the names you enter or the worksheets you create.

The generator does not save entered names in localStorage. When you print or save a worksheet as a PDF, you control where that output goes. Your browser, device, printer, or chosen cloud printing service may handle the output according to its own settings. Keep saved worksheets and printed pages in a place appropriate for the children whose names appear on them.

Local storage and cookies

We use your browser’s localStorage to remember whether Pro features have been unlocked. The entry named nth_pro stores an unlock flag, not a child’s name or payment card details. LocalStorage stays in that browser until it is cleared or removed by browser settings. Clearing site data or switching browsers may remove your remembered access.

Our website code does not set or read cookies. We do not install analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or tracking pixels. We do not collect email addresses through the generator, require email registration, or operate a mailing list. External payment pages have their own data practices, which are separate from these statements about our website.

Google Fonts

The generator uses Google Fonts to display handwriting fonts. Loading those fonts sends requests from your browser to Google. These requests involve connection information, such as your IP address, and browser request information. They do not include the names typed into the worksheet fields. Read the Google Privacy Policy for information about Google’s handling of personal information. This policy page uses system fonts and does not load Google Fonts.

Stripe payments

Stripe processes Pro payments on its checkout page. Information you provide there is handled by Stripe; our generator does not receive or store your full payment card details. Stripe may request an email address and other transaction information. Payment records available to us through Stripe may include contact information needed to manage a purchase or refund. Please review the Stripe Privacy Policy before paying.

Support and ordinary website delivery

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to answer your question or handle your purchase issue, not to add you to marketing lists. Avoid sending children’s names or completed worksheets when a description of the problem will do.

Visiting any hosted website requires connection information to deliver its pages. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request information, such as an IP address and requested URL. That is separate from worksheet processing: names entered into the generator are not included in those requests by our code.

Questions and updates

Contact hello@nametracinghub.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent us. We will update this page if our practices change and show the revision date above. Adults should supervise children’s use and decide how generated worksheets are stored or shared.
