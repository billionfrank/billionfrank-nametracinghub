Refund Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Name Tracing Hub Pro is a digital product purchased with a one-time payment. Delivery means making the paid generator features available after checkout; we do not ship a physical item. Worksheets are generated in your browser, and PDF copies are created through your browser’s print or save options.

Our general policy

Because digital features are available for immediate use, purchases are generally non-refundable after delivery. This includes a change of mind after receiving working access or deciding that you no longer need the worksheets. Please try the free generator and review the Pro feature list before buying so you can decide whether the tool suits your household or classroom.

This general rule does not replace the technical-problem refund commitment below. It also does not limit any refund or other consumer rights that apply by law. If you believe your situation falls outside the examples on this page, you may still contact us and explain what happened.

When we provide a full refund

If a worksheet PDF created using the generator cannot be opened, or if your completed payment does not unlock Pro features, email hello@nametracinghub.com. For either of these problems, we will handle your request and issue a full refund within 48 hours of receiving your email. This means 48 hours, not two business days.

The full refund covers the amount charged for the affected Pro purchase. You do not have to accept store credit or a replacement product instead. We may offer help with the problem, but accepting troubleshooting is not a condition of receiving the refund promised for these issues.

How to contact us

Use the subject line “Refund request” and briefly explain whether your PDF will not open or your paid features remain locked. Include the purchase date and a Stripe receipt or transaction reference if you have one. These details help us locate the correct payment promptly. If you cannot find your receipt, send your request anyway and tell us what purchase information you have.

You may include your browser name and any error message you see. Please do not send full card numbers, security codes, passwords, or children’s personal information. A written description is usually enough to start. If you choose to send a screenshot, remove names and other sensitive details first.

Refund timing and payment method

We issue refunds through Stripe to the original payment method. Our 48-hour commitment refers to our handling of the request and submission of the full refund. Your bank or payment provider controls when the credit appears on your statement, so the money may become visible after that period.

We will confirm by email when the refund has been issued. If the credit does not appear, reply to that confirmation so we can help you check the refund status. You will not be charged a refund administration fee by Name Tracing Hub.

Related information

For permitted worksheet uses, read our Terms of Use. For information about local browser storage, Stripe, and support correspondence, read our Privacy Policy. All refund questions can be sent directly to hello@nametracinghub.com.
